I'm a philosopher of science mainly interested in the history and philosophy of evolutionary biology (with an emphasis on evo-devo and evolvability research), as well as science-society dynamics in synthetic biology, COVID-19 science, climate change sciences, and the biology of reproduction. More generally, I am interested in interdisciplinarity and theory integration, the role of values in science, new materialism, and emerging forms of scientific realism.
