Emily Parke is an Associate Professor of Philosophy at 
[Waipapa Taumata Rau | The University of Auckland](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/philosophy.html). Before moving there she grew up in Seattle, got her BA at Reed College, worked for a synthetic biology company in Italy, and completed her PhD at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://philosophy.sas.upenn.edu/).

She works on various topics in the philosophy of science and philosophy of biology, drawing on a mixed background in philosophy and biology. Her recent work focuses on microbiology, astrobiology, and various topics at the local interface of philosophy and science in Aotearoa; you can find out more on her website, linked below.
She is an Associate Editor at [Biology & Philosophy](https://link.springer.com/journal/10539) and Assistant Director of [Te Ao Mārama—Centre for Fundamental Inquiry](https://www.teaomarama.auckland.ac.nz/).
